# AI Use

Use the course prompting pattern when AI is allowed:

```text
Context -> Attempt -> Allowed Help -> Constraints -> Questions -> Output
```

In your AI notes or disclosure, identify the closest AI use classification:

- **AI Assistance**: AI supported critique, explanation, review, or thinking.
- **AI Enabled / Collaboration**: AI helped create, revise, compare, or complete a targeted part after your manual framing.
- **AI Embedded**: AI was used inside a tool or system that directly supported the workflow.

Start manually.

Do not use AI until after you complete your manual issue list and revision plan.

Allowed AI uses:

- ask AI to critique your revision plan
- ask AI to identify which issue is most worth revising now
- ask AI to point out a change that may add unnecessary complexity
- ask AI to help identify boundary questions
- ask AI to suggest a reviewer question

Not allowed:

- asking AI to redesign the whole model
- replacing your model with an AI-generated model
- accepting AI changes without explaining your decision
- writing SQL

If you use AI, complete:

```text
ai_assistance_notes.md
```

Your notes should explain:

- the AI use classification
- what you completed manually first
- the context, attempt, allowed help, constraints, questions, and output format in your prompt
- the exact prompt you used
- what AI suggested or produced
- what you accepted, rejected, or changed
- what you verified yourself
- why the final decision remained yours

# Instructor Notes

This assignment should teach students that revision is a modeling skill, not a correction penalty.

The key teaching target is:

```text
prior evidence -> issue identification -> focused revision -> pattern consideration -> change log -> boundary discipline
```

Do not let students jump to:

- redesigning the entire model
- adding complexity to look advanced
- SQL
- treating every pattern as required

Useful feedback patterns:

- Good: revision is evidence-based, tradeoff is named, deferrals show scope control, pattern use is purposeful.
- Improvements: vague issue list, revision not connected to evidence, excessive redesign, quality self-assessment has no examples.
- Suggestions: narrow the revision, document one deferred question, explain why a pattern fits or does not fit, compare the revised artifact to the earlier version.
