# CAPSTONE MODELING DEFENSE

**Week 8 - Final Database Design Evidence and Defense**

---

# Context

The capstone is your final modeling defense for this course.

You will present a database design concept, organize the required evidence, and explain why your design choices fit the system idea. This is still a modeling course artifact. You are not expected to build a full production database application.

Your final work should show that you can reason about scope, entities, relationships, rules, representation choices, and responsible AI assistance.

SQL is not part of this course capstone.

---

# Objective

Create and defend a final database model.

Your work should demonstrate:

- a clear system scope
- an ERD or equivalent visual model
- business rules and constraints
- relational/table-shaped model evidence
- NoSQL/document evidence if used
- representation choice reasoning
- a final design claim
- honest limitations
- complete AI assistance disclosure

# AI Use

Use the course prompting pattern when AI is allowed:

```text
Context -> Attempt -> Allowed Help -> Constraints -> Questions -> Output
```

In your AI notes or disclosure, identify the closest AI use classification:

- **AI Assistance**: AI supported critique, explanation, review, or thinking.
- **AI Enabled / Collaboration**: AI helped create, revise, compare, or complete a targeted part after your manual framing.
- **AI Embedded**: AI was used inside a tool or system that directly supported the workflow.

AI assistance is allowed, but it must be disclosed.

AI can help with:

- critique
- brainstorming
- checking for missing evidence
- improving wording
- generating a draft structure for Pathway 2 compressed design
- identifying questions to verify

AI cannot replace:

- your final design judgment
- your explanation of why the model fits
- your evidence selection
- your responsibility for errors or unsupported claims

Every student must complete:

```text
ai_assistance_disclosure.md
```

If no AI was used, state that clearly in the disclosure file.

If you choose Pathway 2, your disclosure should be more detailed because you are likely using AI to help accelerate a new design.

# Instructor Notes

This capstone should assess whether students can assemble and defend a coherent model, not whether they can build a production database.

The key teaching target is:

```text
pathway choice -> evidence index -> scope -> visual model -> rules -> representation evidence -> defense claim -> limitation -> AI disclosure
```

Do not let students jump to:

- SQL
- production implementation
- unsupported claims about database superiority
- hiding AI-generated design work
- submitting a pile of prior artifacts without a capstone evidence index

Useful feedback patterns:

- Good: evidence index is complete, design claim is supported, limitations are honest, AI disclosure shows verification and ownership.
- Improvements: scope is too broad, visual model is hard to inspect, relational/document consideration is superficial, final claim overreaches.
- Suggestions: narrow the system boundary, identify the three strongest evidence items, add one missing business rule, clarify what AI helped with and what the student personally verified.
