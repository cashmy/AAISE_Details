# Database Concept Options - Course 123
## Modern Data Modeling for Systems

---

## Student Assignment Concepts
Students can select from these 4 database concepts for their assignments:

### 1. Student/Course/Schedule Tracker
- **Complexity:** High
- **Key Relationships:** Many-to-many (students to courses), temporal data (schedules, semesters)
- **Entities:** Students, Courses, Instructors, Schedules, Rooms, Semesters
- **Use Case:** Academic scheduling and tracking system

### 2. Customer/Orders/Invoicing
- **Complexity:** Medium-High
- **Key Relationships:** Business process modeling, transactional relationships
- **Entities:** Customers, Orders, OrderItems, Products, Invoices, Payments
- **Use Case:** Retail business transaction system

### 3. Books/Library
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Key Relationships:** Cataloging system, author/title relationships
- **Entities:** Books, Authors, Categories, Patrons, Loans, Reservations
- **Use Case:** Library management system

### 4. Music Collection
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Key Relationships:** Hierarchical patterns (genres, artists, albums)
- **Entities:** Artists, Albums, Songs, Genres, Playlists, Users
- **Use Case:** Personal music collection management

## Instructor Demo Concepts

### Lecture Demo: E-Commerce with Reviews/Ratings
- **Purpose:** Progressive demo for lecture demonstrations
- **Complexity:** Medium
- **Key Features:** Relational structure with customer/product/order relationships, extends to AI-ready search concepts
- **Entities:** Customers, Products, Orders, Reviews, Categories
- **Use Case:** E-commerce system with product recommendations and feedback
- **Availability:** Used in lectures, NOT available for student assignments

### Progressive Demo: Project Management System
- **Purpose:** Complex progressive demo similar to PageForge approach from Course 118
- **Complexity:** High
- **Key Features:** Complex relationships, status tracking, user roles, comprehensive system modeling
- **Entities:** Projects, Tasks, Users, Teams, Milestones, TimeEntries, Comments
- **Use Case:** Full project management application
- **Availability:** Used as comprehensive reference example, milestones backfilled into weekly structure

## Capstone Options
For the Week 8 capstone project, students can:

- Include AI-ready data considerations

## Concept Selection Rationale

**Student Assignment Concepts:**
- Cover different complexity levels (medium to high)
- Represent diverse real-world scenarios (academic, business, library, personal)
- Support different relationship patterns (many-to-many, hierarchical, transactional)
- Provide meaningful modeling challenges without being overwhelming

**Instructor Demo Concepts:**
- E-commerce: Relatable business context, good for lecture progression, extends to AI concepts
- Project Management: Complex enough for comprehensive reference, similar PageForge pattern success

**Concept Separation:**
- Student concepts distinct from instructor concepts to prevent template copying
- Instructor concepts provide working examples without limiting student creativity
- Capstone flexibility allows synthesis of learned concepts with original thinking
