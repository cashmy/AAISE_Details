# Week 5 Demo Package

Course: `10-152-123 Modern Data Modeling for Systems`

Week 5 has two separate assignments:

- A5 Normalization and Anomaly Analysis
- A6 Business Rules and Performance Tradeoffs

The week should stay at the modeling-judgment level. Students reason about structure, maintenance risks, business rules, and performance pressure. They do not write SQL.
