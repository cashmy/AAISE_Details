## Additional Idea to Preserve

One additional concept surfaced late in the session and should be preserved for
follow-up design work:

- incorporate a more explicit **personal resume / job placement preparation**
  process into the bridge and/or future AAISE direction

This should likely include:

- resume creation oriented toward actual software and AI-era job placement
- ATS-aware resume constraints for stronger real-world viability
- how to use AI-assistance responsibly when drafting or refining resumes
- the difference between older resume-content expectations and the newer
  content employers may now value more strongly
- how students should present AI-assisted, AI-injected, and traditional work in
  professional documents
- how portfolio, project, and skills language may need to be updated for the
  AI-augmented labor market

This appears to be a meaningful employability-layer addition rather than a
minor side note, and should be revisited explicitly in a later session.
