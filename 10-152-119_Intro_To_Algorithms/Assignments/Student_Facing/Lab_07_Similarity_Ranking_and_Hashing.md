# LAB 7 - SIMILARITY, RANKING, AND HASHING

**Week 7 - Similarity, Clustering, Recommendation, and Hashing**

---

# Context

Many AI and data systems begin with algorithmic choices: how to represent data,
measure similarity, rank options, group items, or verify identity.

In this lab, you will build a small visible data/AI bridge activity.

---

# Objective

Apply one introductory AI/data algorithm idea and explain its assumptions and
limits.

Your work should demonstrate:

- representation of small data
- similarity, ranking, grouping, or hashing behavior
- visible evidence
- explanation of what the result can and cannot claim

---

# Task

Choose one option:

1. **Similarity Ranking**
   - Compare items using shared tags or numeric features.
2. **Simple Clustering**
   - Group points or items based on closeness.
3. **Tiny Recommendation**
   - Rank possible suggestions based on a user profile.
4. **Hashing Demonstration**
   - Use hashing to show lookup, identity, or integrity checking.

Your instructor may assign one option for consistency.

---

# Requirements

Your submission must include:

1. A small data set with at least `6` items.
2. A description of how the data is represented.
3. A Python implementation or clear simulation of the selected algorithm idea.
4. A table, chart, matrix, grouping, or hash comparison as visible evidence.
5. An explanation of at least `2` assumptions.
6. An explanation of at least `1` limitation or risk.
7. A short statement connecting the activity to AI, analytics, or data modeling.

---

# Evidence Requirements

Choose the evidence format that fits your option.

Possible formats:

- similarity matrix
- ranking table
- cluster grouping table
- scatter plot
- hash value comparison
- before/after data representation table

---

# AI Use

You may use AI as a comparison partner after you have selected the data,
representation, and algorithm idea.

Allowed AI uses:

- ask AI to critique your assumptions
- ask AI to suggest a different representation
- ask AI to explain why your result might be misleading
- ask AI to generate a small variant only after your first version works

If AI generates code or an alternate method, you must validate it against your
own evidence.

---

# Submission Requirements

Submit your files or written response as instructed.

Your submission should include:

- selected option
- data set

- implementation or simulation
- visible evidence
- assumptions
- limitation or risk
- AI/data connection
- AI-use note, if applicable

# Reflection / Explanation

Answer briefly:

> What choice did you make about representation, and how did it affect the
> result?

If you used AI:

> What did AI help you question about your algorithm or data?
