## Key Takeaway:
> Students who genuinely engage with this course, complete the labs, explain their reasoning, and learn to use AI responsibly will likely be operating in the top 15-20% of junior applicants in terms of algorithmic reasoning, AI-assisted problem analysis, and ability to explain technical tradeoffs.

Title: Why This Course Matters

Slide text: 
```text
Many junior developers can say:

"I made it work."

This course trains you to say:

"Here is the problem I solved.
Here are the assumptions I made.
Here is the strategy I chose.
Here is the evidence that it works.
Here is the tradeoff I accepted.
Here is how I used AI without surrendering judgment."
```

Instructor Notes: 
``` text
This is the professional difference the course is aiming at. Students are not
expected to become advanced algorithm specialists in eight weeks. They are
expected to develop the reasoning posture that lets them work above the normal
junior level: frame the problem, test the solution, explain the tradeoffs, and
use AI as a tool without letting it replace their thinking.
```
