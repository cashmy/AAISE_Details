# Course 10-152-119: Introduction to Algorithms Grading Summary

Each assignment may be assessed in up to **12 rubric categories**. Each category is scored from **0 to 4 points**, so a fully assessed assignment is worth up to **48 points**.

Your course grade includes **9 graded items** organized into two categories:

| Category | Graded items | Weight in final grade |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Homework/Lab | Labs 1-7 (7 labs) | 63.64% |
| Final Assessment | Part 1: Applied Solution Set; Part 2: Explanation Defense | 36.36% |

Each final-assessment part counts twice as much as a regular lab. The two final parts are weighted equally within the Final Assessment category.

## Instructor / LMS Setup Notes

Use the same 0-4 master rubric for the seven labs and the two final-assessment parts, selecting the rows that are relevant to each graded item. Assign Labs 1-7 to the **Homework/Lab** category. Assign the Applied Solution Set and Explanation Defense to the **Final Assessment** category, with equal weighting between the two final parts.

The 63.64% / 36.36% category weights produce the same result as treating each lab as 48 points and each final-assessment part as 96 points. This is equivalent to **528 possible points**:

| Assignment group | Graded items | Equivalent points per item | Equivalent total points |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Homework/Lab | Labs 1-7 (7 labs) | 48 | 336 |
| Final Assessment | Part 1 and Part 2 (2 items) | 96 | 192 |
| **Course total** | **9 graded items** |  | **528** |

This LMS setup intentionally uses an equal 50/50 split between the two final parts. It supersedes the 70/30 suggested split in `Assignments/FINAL-AL_Final_Algorithmic_Reasoning_Assessment.md`.

Equivalent integer point thresholds are A: 476-528, B: 423-475, C: 370-422, D: 317-369, and F: 0-316.
