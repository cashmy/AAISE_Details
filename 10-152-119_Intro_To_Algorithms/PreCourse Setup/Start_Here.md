## **What This Course Is About**

This course is about algorithmic problem solving.

That means you will practice how to:

* define problems clearly
* choose a reasonable solution strategy
* use data structures appropriately
* test whether a solution works
* compare tradeoffs between approaches
* explain your reasoning and evidence

You are not expected to memorize every algorithm perfectly. You are expected to
learn how to reason through problems, test your work, and explain why an
approach makes sense.

---

## **Weekly Class Format**

Most weeks in this course follow a lecture-demo-lab rhythm:

* **Concept frame**
  * We introduce the week's main idea and why it matters.
* **Instructor demo**
  * You will see a related example worked through in class.
* **Lab work**
  * You will apply the same kind of thinking to a different problem.
* **Evidence and explanation**
  * You will show how your solution behaves and explain your reasoning.

The demo and the lab will be related, but they will not be identical. The goal
is transfer, not copying.

AI may be used in this course, but it must be used responsibly.

The basic pattern is:

```text
frame first -> attempt or trace -> use AI within the boundary ->
test with evidence -> compare and explain -> revise responsibly
```

AI can help with explanation, examples, debugging, comparison, or code revision
when the assignment allows it. AI may not replace your responsibility to
understand, test, and explain your own work.

## **Step 6 - Understand the Final Assessment**

This course does not have a traditional capstone project.

Instead, the course ends with a two-part final assessment:

```text
Part 1 - Applied Solution Set
Part 2 - Personalized Explanation Defense
```

In Part 1, you will complete several bounded algorithmic tasks and submit your
solutions with evidence.

In Part 2, you will answer questions about your own submitted work. The goal is
not to trick you. The goal is to verify that you understand what you submitted,
including assumptions, tests, tradeoffs, limitations, and any AI assistance.

More specific final instructions will be provided later in the course.
