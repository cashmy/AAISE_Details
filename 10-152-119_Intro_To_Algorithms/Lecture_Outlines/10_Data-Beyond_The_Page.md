# 🧭 **WEEK 10 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 10 — Data Flow & Asynchronous Thinking*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about understanding how data moves into your application.”

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* Data doesn’t arrive instantly
* Programs must wait for results
* Async = delayed response
* UI updates after data arrives

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “Why is this undefined?” → data not loaded yet
* “Why does this work sometimes?” → timing issues
* “I don’t understand the structure” → JSON confusion

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

* “Where does the data come from?”
* “When does it arrive?”
* “What does it look like?”
* “What do I do with it after?”

---

## **5. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, focus on making your data usage clearer and more reliable.”

Examples:

* improve display
* simplify data handling
* reduce confusion

---

## **6. Closing Reinforcement**

> “Understanding data flow is key to building real applications.”

---
