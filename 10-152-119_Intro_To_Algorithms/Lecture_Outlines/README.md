# 10-152-119 Lecture Outlines - v2

## Purpose

This folder contains the lecture outline system for `10-152-119 Algorithmic
Problem Solving`.

The outlines should follow the compressed 8-week course model used in
`10-152-117 Python Programming`: live class framing, short concept blocks,
visible demonstrations, guided lab movement, and end-of-session readiness
checks.

This folder should not inherit the special `10-152-118 HTML/CSS/JavaScript`
midweek video lecture pattern. Algorithms does not include a required video or
asynchronous lecture element in the IIM.

## Outline Creation Guidance

Weekly outlines are now drafted from the stabilized lab/final structure.
Additional day-specific or demo-specific outlines may be created after demo
files and final lab packets are built.

Each outline should anchor:

- the readiness target
- the visible demo or trace
- the lab bridge
- the algorithmic evidence students must produce
- the AI involvement level for that session
- the Review / Bridge From Prior Week section for Weeks 2-8

Week 8 is a final synthesis and assessment outline, not a full lab outline.
