# 5. Course Positioning

Students now begin naming strategy families and comparing solutions beyond whether they run.

---

# 6. Core Concepts

- Iteration: repeated steps using loops.
- Recursion: a function solving a smaller version of the problem.
- Base case: where recursion stops.
- Brute force: trying possibilities directly.
- Greedy strategy: choosing what looks best now.

---

# 7. Algorithm Visibility / Demo Plan

Demo nested donation envelopes.

Show:

- iterative approach for flattened data
- recursive approach for nested data
- call trace or decision tree
- strategy comparison table

Evidence:

- recursive call trace
- comparison of correctness/readability/growth

---

# 8. Hands-On / Lab Bridge

Students begin Lab 5 with a different problem.

Their goal is not to prove one strategy is always best. Their goal is to explain which strategy fits this problem.

---

# 9. Common Mistakes / Watch-Fors

- missing base case
- tracing only the first recursive call
- calling one strategy "better" without criteria
- using AI-generated code without explaining the strategy

---

# 10. AI Use Frame

AI may suggest a second strategy after students frame their first approach.

If AI generates code, students must test it and explain the strategy in their own words.

---

# 11. Explain / Checkpoint Questions

- What is the base case?
- What makes this problem recursive or iterative?
- Which strategy is easier to explain?
- Which strategy may scale better?
- What evidence supports your comparison?

---

# 12. End-of-Class Success Check

By the end of this session, students should be able to compare two strategies using evidence and context.
