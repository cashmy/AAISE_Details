# Three Ways Businesses Use AI

**Alternate Series Title:**  
_Understanding AI-Assisted, AI-Enabled, and AI-Embedded Workplace Applications_

## Series Description

This three-session continuing-education series helps employees understand and apply three common classifications of workplace AI: AI-Assisted work, AI-Enabled Collaboration, and AI-Embedded applications.

Industrial employers provide the primary context, while examples and activities remain relevant to the broader business community. Each session stands alone and begins with a brief overview of all three classifications. Participants may complete one session or take the full series as a progressive learning pathway.

## Working Classifications

- **AI-Assisted:** An employee uses AI to support a defined task while retaining control of the process and final result.
- **AI-Enabled Collaboration:** An employee works iteratively with AI across multiple stages of a task, problem, or project.
- **AI-Embedded:** AI capabilities are built into an application or platform used to perform workplace activities.

These classifications are working definitions for instruction and may differ from terminology used by individual employers or software providers.

## Sessions

1. **AI Assistance and Better Prompting** - 3-4 hours
2. **AI-Enabled Workplace Collaboration** - 3-4 hours
3. **Using AI-Embedded Business Apps** - 1-2 hours

## Standalone Design

No session is a prerequisite for another. Each session includes:

- A concise overview of the three classifications
- Examples from industrial and general business settings
- Responsible-use and human-oversight guidance
- Familiar product references without product-specific instruction
- An activity or decision framework appropriate to the session

## Product Approach

The series is vendor-neutral. Instructors may reference familiar general-purpose AI tools and AI features found in common office, communication, document, HR, customer-management, and business applications. Product-specific setup, licensing, configuration, and technical integration are outside the core series.
