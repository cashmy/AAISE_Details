# AI-Enabled Workplace Collaboration

**Alternate Title:**  
_Working Iteratively with AI on Workplace Tasks and Projects_

## Course Description

This three- to four-hour continuing-education workshop explores AI-Enabled Collaboration: an employee working iteratively with AI across multiple stages of a task, problem, or project while retaining human judgment, control, and accountability.

Participants move beyond one-time requests and learn how to break work into stages, provide relevant context, review intermediate results, redirect the process, and assemble a final work product. Examples are drawn primarily from industrial organizations while remaining applicable to the broader business community.

The session stands alone. It includes a concise prompting review so prior completion of an AI prompting course is not required.

## Course Structure: Approximately 3-4 Hours

1. **The Three Classifications of Workplace AI - 25-30 minutes**  
   Distinguish among AI-Assisted work, AI-Enabled Collaboration, and AI-Embedded applications, and establish the focus of the workshop.

2. **From a Single Prompt to Collaboration - 30-40 minutes**  
   Compare one-time AI assistance with an iterative process involving questions, revisions, alternatives, critique, and refinement.

3. **Context, Task Stages, and Prompting Review - 35-45 minutes**  
   Break a project into manageable stages, provide relevant context without exposing protected information, and use clear instructions at each stage.

4. **Collaborative Workplace Applications - 45-60 minutes**  
   Apply an iterative process to planning, document development, process analysis, training preparation, communication, or another fictional workplace scenario.

5. **Quality, Boundaries, and Human Oversight - 25-30 minutes**  
   Manage inaccurate output, context loss, bias, confidentiality, overreliance, and responsibility for decisions and final work.

6. **Guided Exercise and Action Plan - 20-25 minutes**  
   Complete a short collaborative activity and identify one appropriate workplace process for further exploration.

The shorter format uses a compact instructor-led scenario. The four-hour format allows a more complete guided exercise, discussion, and a short break.

## Potential Applications

- Developing and refining a project outline
- Preparing training or onboarding materials
- Exploring alternatives during problem-solving
- Organizing a multistep communication plan
- Reviewing a document from several perspectives
- Converting notes into a structured first draft
- Developing questions, checklists, and review criteria

## Learning Outcomes

Participants will be able to:

- Explain how AI-Enabled Collaboration differs from one-time AI assistance.
- Divide a workplace task into appropriate stages.
- Provide and manage context during an iterative AI interaction.
- Critique, redirect, and refine intermediate AI output.
- Maintain human responsibility for final work and decisions.
- Identify an appropriate low-risk collaborative use case.

## Product Approach

Familiar AI products may be referenced, but the collaborative methods are vendor-neutral. Product-specific automation, integrations, and system access are outside the course scope.
