# AI Assistance and Better Prompting

**Alternate Title:**  
_Using Generative AI to Support Everyday Workplace Tasks_

## Course Description

This three- to four-hour continuing-education workshop introduces AI-Assisted work: using generative AI to support a defined employee-directed task while the employee retains control, judgment, and responsibility for the final result.

Participants learn practical prompting techniques and apply them to common industrial, administrative, HR, operations, and business tasks. The course emphasizes task selection, clear instructions, iterative improvement, output verification, confidentiality, and human oversight.

The workshop is designed for the broader business community, with industrial workplace examples serving as the primary context.

## Course Structure: Approximately 3-4 Hours

1. **The Three Classifications of Workplace AI - 25-30 minutes**  
   Distinguish among AI-Assisted work, AI-Enabled Collaboration, and AI-Embedded applications, and establish the scope of this workshop.

2. **Choosing Appropriate Tasks - 25-30 minutes**  
   Identify suitable low-risk tasks and recognize activities involving confidential data, high-impact decisions, or organizational restrictions.

3. **Prompting Fundamentals - 50-60 minutes**  
   Practice defining a role, task, context, constraints, desired format, and quality expectations. Learn how follow-up instructions improve an initial response.

4. **Reviewing and Verifying Results - 30-35 minutes**  
   Check output for accuracy, unsupported claims, omissions, bias, tone, and alignment with the original task.

5. **Applied Workplace Practice - 40-60 minutes**  
   Use fictional or nonconfidential information to draft, summarize, organize, compare, revise, or explain workplace materials.

6. **Personal Application Plan - 10-15 minutes**  
   Select one appropriate task for further exploration and define the required human review and organizational approval.

The shorter format uses instructor demonstrations and limited practice. The four-hour workshop allows additional exercises, discussion, and a short break.

## Potential Applications

- Drafting routine communications
- Summarizing nonconfidential information
- Creating outlines and checklists
- Revising tone, clarity, and organization
- Brainstorming questions or alternatives
- Converting information into a requested format
- Preparing preliminary training or meeting materials

## Learning Outcomes

Participants will be able to:

- Explain the defining characteristics of AI-Assisted work.
- Select appropriate workplace tasks for AI assistance.
- Write prompts containing sufficient context, constraints, and output requirements.
- Improve results through follow-up instructions.
- Evaluate AI-generated work before using or sharing it.
- Identify confidential or high-risk information that should not be entered into an unapproved tool.

## Product Approach

Examples may reference familiar general-purpose AI products, but prompting techniques are taught in a vendor-neutral manner. Participants are not required to use a specific product.
