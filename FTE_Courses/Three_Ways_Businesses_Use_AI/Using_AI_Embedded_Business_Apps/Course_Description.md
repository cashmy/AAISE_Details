# Using AI-Embedded Business Apps

**Alternate Title:**  
_Understanding and Evaluating AI Features in Workplace Applications_

## Course Description

This one- to two-hour continuing-education course introduces AI-Embedded applications: workplace software containing built-in AI features that assist users within the application.

Participants learn how embedded AI differs from stand-alone AI assistance and AI-enabled collaboration, what questions to ask before using an AI-enhanced feature, and how data access, permissions, accuracy, security, and human oversight affect responsible use.

Industrial applications provide the primary context, while the course remains useful across the broader business community. Familiar products may be referenced as examples, but instruction remains vendor-neutral and avoids feature-specific training.

## Common Core: Approximately 60-90 Minutes

1. **The Three Classifications of Workplace AI - 15 minutes**  
   Distinguish among AI-Assisted work, AI-Enabled Collaboration, and AI-Embedded applications.

2. **Recognizing Embedded AI - 15-20 minutes**  
   Identify common ways AI appears within office, communication, document, HR, customer-management, industrial, and other business applications.

3. **Data, Permissions, and Security - 15-20 minutes**  
   Consider what information a feature may access, where output may appear, who can view it, and what organizational approval may be required.

4. **Reviewing AI-Generated Results - 15-20 minutes**  
   Evaluate accuracy, completeness, bias, context, and whether a person must approve the result or decision.

## Optional Pathways

The common core can be followed by either or both of these pathways, depending on employer and participant needs.

### Pathway A: Employee Use

Focus on recognizing and responsibly using AI features already available in approved workplace applications.

Potential topics include:

- Confirming that a feature is approved for use
- Understanding available controls and notices
- Reviewing generated summaries, drafts, recommendations, or classifications
- Avoiding confidential or inappropriate use
- Knowing when to stop and request guidance

### Pathway B: Organizational Evaluation

Focus on questions leaders and staff should consider before enabling, purchasing, or encouraging use of an embedded AI feature.

Potential topics include:

- Business purpose and expected value
- Data access and permission boundaries
- Vendor and security review responsibilities
- Human oversight and high-impact decisions
- Pilot testing, employee communication, and ongoing review

The 60-minute version presents the common core as an awareness briefing. The 90- to 120-minute version adds one pathway, scenarios, and facilitated discussion. A later decision can determine whether employee use, organizational evaluation, or both should become the standard offering.

## Learning Outcomes

Participants will be able to:

- Explain what makes an AI capability embedded within a business application.
- Distinguish embedded AI from stand-alone assistance and collaboration.
- Identify questions concerning data access, permissions, security, and accuracy.
- Recognize when human review or organizational approval is required.
- Evaluate an embedded AI feature from either a user or organizational perspective.

## Outside the Course Scope

- Technical installation or configuration
- Software integration or API development
- Product certification or procurement recommendations
- Access to employer systems or confidential records
- Automated employment or other high-impact decisions
