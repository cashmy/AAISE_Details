# AI Hiring for Industrial HR

**Alternate Title:**
_Using AI to Improve Candidate Discovery, Resume Screening, and Hiring Efficiency in Industrial Organizations_

**Course Description**

This continuing-education course prepares HR professionals, hiring managers, and operational leaders in industrial organizations to use AI more effectively during candidate discovery and resume review.

Participants will explore practical ways to identify qualified candidates, reduce administrative workload, improve screening consistency, and shorten hiring timelines. The course also addresses responsible AI use, including human oversight, bias, privacy, and employment-related risk.

---

**Target Audience**

Current employees working in:

- Human resources and talent acquisition
- Manufacturing and production operations
- Logistics, warehousing, and transportation
- Construction and skilled trades
- Energy, utilities, and field services
- Workforce planning and organizational leadership

**Core Business Value**

The strongest positioning is not simply “AI for recruiting.” It is:

> Helping understaffed industrial HR teams find qualified candidates sooner, reduce avoidable hiring costs, and make more consistent decisions while retaining human oversight.

That framing connects the technology directly to the business concerns driving enrollment.
