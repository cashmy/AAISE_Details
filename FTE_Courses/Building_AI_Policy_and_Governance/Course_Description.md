# Building AI Policy and Governance

**Alternate Title:**  
_Developing Practical AI Policy, Security, and Governance Documents for Business_

## Course Description

This two-hour continuing-education workshop helps business leaders, supervisors, HR professionals, administrative staff, and technology personnel begin developing practical guidance for responsible workplace AI use.

Industrial employers provide the primary business context, but the principles apply across the broader business community. Participants examine acceptable use, confidential information, security, human oversight, accountability, and review practices while building a starter AI governance document for their organization.

The workshop is educational and does not replace legal, privacy, cybersecurity, or regulatory review. Final organizational policies should be reviewed by the appropriate authorized professionals.

## Target Audience

- Business owners and organizational leaders
- HR and workforce-development professionals
- Supervisors and department managers
- Administrative and operations personnel
- Information technology and security personnel
- Employees assigned to AI policy or governance initiatives

No technical AI background is required.

## Workshop Structure: Approximately 2 Hours

1. **Why AI Governance Matters - 15 minutes**  
   Examine common workplace uses of AI, organizational responsibilities, and the business risks created by unclear expectations.

2. **Information, Privacy, and Security Boundaries - 20 minutes**  
   Identify categories of information that require protection and consider when an AI tool, account, or proposed use may be inappropriate.

3. **Core Elements of an AI Policy - 25 minutes**  
   Review purpose, scope, approved and prohibited uses, human oversight, output verification, accountability, incident reporting, and policy-review provisions.

4. **Starter-Document Workshop - 35 minutes**  
   Use a guided template to begin an AI acceptable-use and governance document appropriate to the participant's organization.

5. **Review, Adoption, and Next Steps - 15 minutes**  
   Identify required stakeholders, unresolved questions, approval responsibilities, employee communication, and a schedule for future review.

The remaining time supports a short break, discussion, and transitions between activities.

## Participant Deliverable

Each participant will leave with a starter document that may include:

- Purpose and scope
- Definitions and levels of AI use
- Approved and prohibited uses
- Confidential and restricted information
- Human review and decision responsibility
- Accuracy and source-verification expectations
- Security and incident-reporting procedures
- Ownership, approval, and review schedule

## Learning Outcomes

Participants will be able to:

- Explain why workplace AI requires organizational guidance.
- Identify common privacy, security, accuracy, and accountability concerns.
- Distinguish among acceptable, restricted, and prohibited AI uses.
- Draft the core sections of a starter AI governance document.
- Identify the organizational stakeholders required to review and approve the document.

## Product Approach

The workshop is vendor-neutral. Familiar AI products and AI features in office, communication, HR, and business applications may be referenced as examples, but the course does not provide product-specific configuration or implementation instruction.

## Outside the Course Scope

- Legal or regulatory advice
- Final policy approval or certification
- Cybersecurity testing or technical system assessment
- AI-system integration or deployment
- Product procurement or vendor selection
- Processing confidential organizational records
