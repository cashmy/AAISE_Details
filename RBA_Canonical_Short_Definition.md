# RBA - CANONICAL SHORT DEFINITION

**Refraction-Based Architecture**

---

## Canonical Definition

Refraction-Based Architecture (RBA) is a **human-governed, AI-amplified design process** in which intent, constraints, and architectural direction are established first; AI is then used as a refraction medium to accelerate drafting, comparison, transformation, and revision; and concrete artifacts are repeatedly tested against reality so that coherence can be preserved through iterative realignment.

---

## Short Practical Version

RBA means:

* the human defines purpose and boundaries first
* AI works inside that governed frame
* outputs are treated as provisional refractions, not autonomous authorship
* reality contact is used to expose drift, gaps, and hidden constraints
* revision and refactoring are expected parts of the process

---

## Ultra-Short Version

**RBA is the practice of governing AI-assisted creation through intent-first framing, bounded refraction, reality contact, and iterative realignment.**

---

## Process Memory Aid

**frame -> refract -> inspect -> refactor -> realign**
