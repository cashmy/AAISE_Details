# Course Position

This course introduces AI in stages rather than treating it as a default tool from the start.

The early part of the course is designed to help students develop a foundation in:

* structure
* styling
* layout
* programming logic

before AI becomes a more strategic development aid.

This supports the course goal of helping students think, build, debug, and explain rather than relying on generation too early.

---

# AI Progression in This Course

## Early Course

In the early phases of the course, AI use should be limited or delayed for normal assignment work so students can build direct understanding of:

* HTML structure
* CSS styling
* layout
* introductory JavaScript logic

## Middle Course

As the course moves into behavior, debugging, and structured JavaScript, AI may be allowed in more bounded ways such as:

* explanation
* comparison
* debugging support
* code organization suggestions

## Later Course

In later phases, especially during more integrated project work and capstone development, AI may be used more strategically to support:

* troubleshooting
* refinement
* extension of features
* structured progress in independent work

Even then, AI does not remove the student's responsibility to understand and explain the system.

---

# Restrictions in This Course

In this course, students may not:

* bypass foundational structure-building work by relying on AI too early
* submit HTML, CSS, or JavaScript they cannot explain
* use AI in ways that skip required manual practice or debugging
* hide meaningful AI use in project work when disclosure is required

---

# Iterative Project Context

Unlike the Python course, this course includes a more continuous iterative project-development structure.

Because of that, AI use later in the course may help students:

* refine existing work
* troubleshoot integration issues
* extend project features

However, students are still expected to preserve authorship through:

* explanation
* revision
* debugging
* clear understanding of what changed and why

---

# Disclosure Expectations in This Course

For major project work and capstone work, students may be asked to disclose and explain AI use.

Students should be prepared to explain:

* what part of the project AI assisted with
* what was generated or suggested
* what was changed, kept, or rejected
* how the student verified behavior and correctness
* what design or logic decisions remained their own

---

# Final Standard for This Course

In `10-152-118 HTML/CSS/JS`, responsible AI use means:

* students still understand the structure and behavior of what they build
* AI is introduced at the right stage rather than replacing foundational learning
* later AI use supports refinement, troubleshooting, and extension without removing student accountability
