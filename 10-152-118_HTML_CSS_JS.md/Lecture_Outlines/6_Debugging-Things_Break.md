# 🧭 **WEEK 6 — MONDAY LECTURE OUTLINE**

**Title:** *Week 6 — Debugging: Understanding and Fixing Problems*

---

## **1. Opening Frame**

> “Up to this point, you’ve been building things that work.
> This week, we focus on what happens when they don’t.”

* Normalize failure
* Remove fear early

---

## **2. Course Positioning**

> “You now have structure, style, layout, and behavior.
> Now you need to understand how to fix them.”

* Debugging is not separate—it applies to everything

---

## **3. Core Concepts**

* What debugging actually is (investigation, not guessing)
* Using the browser console
* Reading error messages
* Tracing code step-by-step
* Isolating problems

---

## **4. Demonstration / Example Layer**

* Show:

  * broken interaction → fix it
* Show:

  * console error → identify cause
* Show:

  * incorrect selector → correct it

👉 Emphasize:

* process, not speed

---

## **5. Common Mistakes / Watch-Fors**

* random guessing
* changing too many things at once
* ignoring error messages
* copying code without understanding

---

## **6. Tuesday Lab Bridge**

> “In lab, your goal is not just to fix problems.
> Your goal is to understand why they happened.”

Focus:

* identify issues
* attempt structured fixes
* observe results

---

## **7. Closing Frame**

> “Every problem contains the information needed to fix it.”

---

---

# 🧭 **WEEK 6 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 6 — Debugging as a Process*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about solving problems systematically—not randomly.”

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* Debugging is a process:

  * observe
  * isolate
  * test
  * confirm

* Errors are signals, not failures

* Code behavior can be traced

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “I changed something and it got worse” → uncontrolled changes
* “I don’t know where to look” → lack of isolation
* “It worked before but not now” → regression

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

* “What changed?”
* “What is the exact error?”
* “What happens step-by-step?”
* “Can I isolate the problem to one place?”

---

## **5. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, focus on explaining your fixes—not just making them.”

Examples:

* describe the issue
* describe how you found it
* describe what fixed it

---

## **6. Closing Reinforcement**

> “Debugging is how developers learn—not just how they fix.”

---
