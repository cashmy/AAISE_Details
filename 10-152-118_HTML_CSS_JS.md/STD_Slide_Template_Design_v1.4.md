# 🧭 **STD — SLIDE TEMPLATE DESIGN (v1.4 — FULL & ATOMIC)**

*SWTC — Concept-Driven Instruction System*

---

# 🧱 1. CORE PRINCIPLE (Non-Negotiable)

> Slides are visual anchors that may include **structured support** when required.

---

## Implications

* One primary idea per slide
* Slides are not full explanations
* Slides support both:

  * live instruction
  * asynchronous review
* Structure and clarity take priority over minimalism alone

---

# 🧠 2. CONTENT LAYERS (Canonical Model)

Every slide is composed of four layers:

---

## 🔹 1. Anchor (Student Layer)

**Definition:**

* The main idea of the slide
* The cognitive entry point

**Characteristics:**

* concise
* memorable
* declarative
* visually dominant

---

## 🔹 2. Support (Structured Reinforcement Layer)

**Definition:**

* Structured content that reinforces the anchor

**Forms:**

* bullet lists
* grouped lists
* comparisons
* sequences
* examples

---

## 🔹 3. Cue (Instructor Layer)

**Definition:**

* Guidance for delivery

**Includes:**

* pacing
* emphasis
* framing
* explanation order

---

## 🔹 4. Visual Intent (Catalyst Layer)

**Definition:**

> A structured directive describing:

* how the idea is visually represented
* what is shown
* why it matters

---

## 🔥 Critical Rule

> If Visual Intent cannot be clearly defined → the slide is incomplete.

---

# 🧱 3. SLIDE SPEC FORMAT (Standardized)

```text id="std_fmt"
### SLIDE X — TYPE

[Anchor Line]
[Optional second line]

Support:
- (optional structured bullets)

---
Cue:
- (delivery guidance)

Visual:
- Layout:
- Content:
  - (elements)
- Purpose:
  - (instructional intent)
```

---

---

## 🔹 3. CONTRAST

---

## 🔹 4. SYSTEM

---

## 🔹 5. COMMON FAILURE

* preempts mistakes

---

## 🔹 6. THINKING TOOL

* installs heuristic

---

## 🔹 7. BRIDGE

* directs action

---

## 🔹 8. CLOSING

* reinforces success condition

---

# 🧱 5. SUPPORT LAYER (FORMALIZED)

---

## 🔹 Definition

> Structured information that reinforces the anchor without overwhelming it

---

## 🔹 Allowed Forms

* bullet lists
* grouped lists
* comparisons
* sequences
* examples

---

## 🔹 Prohibited Forms

* paragraphs
* narrative explanation blocks
* mixed conceptual levels

---

# 🧭 6. BULLET SYSTEM (Integrated into STD)

---

## 🔹 6.1 Purpose-Driven Usage

Bullets must represent:

---

### Type A — Enumeration

### Type B — Comparison

### Type C — Sequence

### Type D — Example Sets

---

## 🔹 6.2 Structural Integrity Rule

> All bullets must follow the same grammatical pattern

---

## 🔹 6.3 Scanability Requirement

> Each bullet must be understandable in ~2 seconds

---

## 🔹 6.4 Density Rule

> No fixed limit on bullet count
> Density is governed by scanability

---

## 🔹 6.5 Grouping Requirement

When needed, bullets must be grouped:

```text id="std_group"
Group Title:
- item
- item

Group Title:
- item
- item
```

---

## 🔹 6.6 Anchor Dominance

> Anchor must remain visually dominant over support

---

# 🧱 7. VISUAL INTENT SYSTEM (v1.2 — FORMALIZED)

---

## 🔹 Definition

Visual Intent defines:

* Layout (spatial arrangement)
* Content (elements shown)
* Purpose (instructional function)

---

## 🔹 Required Format

```text id="std_visual"
Visual:
- Layout:
- Content:
  - (elements)
- Purpose:
  - (what this reveals)
```

---

## 🔹 Visual Layout Types

---

### Split (contrast)

### Stack (layers)

### Flow (sequence)

### Hierarchy (structure)

### Before/After (state)

### Isolated Focus

---

## 🔹 Visual Rules

* one visual concept per slide
* must reinforce idea
* no decorative visuals

---

## 🔹 Diagnostic Rule

> If Layout + Content + Purpose are unclear → refine slide

---

# 🧱 8. GUIDED VISUAL MODE (Special Case)

Used for:

* complex diagrams
* instructor-led explanation

---

## 🔹 Structure

* anchor remains present
* support minimal or omitted
* visual dominates

---

## 🔹 Purpose

* progressive explanation
* guided interpretation

---

# 🧱 9. PRESENTATION MODE LAYER (NEW — v1.4)

Each deck operates in one of three modes:

---

## 🔹 🟦 Pre-Frame Mode (Light Blue)

**Purpose:**

* reframe thinking
* introduce big ideas

**Characteristics:**

* minimal support
* conceptual visuals
* high-level anchors

---

## 🔹 🟦 Concept Mode (Blue)

**Purpose:**

* define and clarify

**Characteristics:**

* moderate support
* structured explanations
* balanced visuals

---

## 🔹 🟪 Detailed Mode (Dark Blue / Purple)

**Purpose:**

* expand understanding
* prepare for execution

**Characteristics:**

* expanded support
* code examples
* walkthrough preparation

---

---

## Visual Style

| --------- | ---------- |

## Instructor Role

| Mode      | Role        |

# 🧱 11. PPT TEMPLATE INTERACTION

---

## STD does:

* define structure
* define meaning

---

## PPT Template does:

* apply styling
* apply layout
* enforce visual consistency

---

## Critical Separation

> STD does not control style
> PPT template does not alter structure

---

---

## Slide Count

* target: 7–10 (concept decks)
* detailed decks may expand

---

## Flow Pattern

1. Frame
2. Define
3. Expand
4. Contrast
5. Apply
6. Bridge

---

# 🧠 13. QUALITY CHECK

Before finalizing:

* Support structured and scannable?
* Visual Intent complete?
* Mode correctly applied?
* Anything unnecessary?
