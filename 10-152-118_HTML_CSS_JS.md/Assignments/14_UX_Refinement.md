# 🧭 ASSIGNMENT 14 — UX & REFINEMENT

**Week 14 — “Make It Better to Use”**

---

## 🔹 Context

Your application now:

* works correctly
* handles data and state
* performs efficiently
* avoids common failure issues

This week focuses on improving the **experience of using your application**.

---

## 🔹 Objective

Refine your application by improving:

* usability
* clarity of interaction
* visual consistency
* overall user experience

---

# 🧱 ITERATION 1 — BUILD (Tuesday Lab)

### 🔹 Task

Identify areas where your application can be improved for usability and clarity.

---

### 🔹 Requirements

You must:

* Identify at least **2 UX issues**, such as:

  * unclear layout
  * confusing interactions
  * poor spacing or readability
  * lack of feedback to the user

* Implement improvements such as:

  * clearer labels or instructions
  * improved spacing and alignment
  * visual feedback (messages, changes, indicators)

---

### 🔹 Optional (Tooling — Controlled Use)

You may use a library such as **Bootstrap** for layout or styling improvements.

**Only use it if:**

* you understand what it is doing
* you can explain how it affects your layout

---

### 🔹 Focus

* clarity for the user
* ease of interaction
* visual organization

---

### 🔹 Expectation

This is not about adding features—it is about improving what already exists.

---

# 🧠 CONCEPT FOCUS (Wednesday)

* What makes an interface easy or difficult to use
* The role of layout, spacing, and feedback in UX
* Consistency and predictability in interaction
* The difference between functionality and usability
* Using tools (like Bootstrap) as acceleration—not replacement

---

# 🔧 ITERATION 2 — REFINE (Thursday Lab)

### 🔹 Task

Improve and polish your application further.

---

### 🔹 Requirements

* Ensure:

  * interactions are clear and predictable
  * layout is consistent across pages

* Improve:

  * user feedback (messages, updates, responses)
  * visual hierarchy (what stands out vs what doesn’t)

* Add at least:

  * one improvement that makes your application easier to use

---

### 🔹 Focus

* polish
* clarity
* consistency

---

# 📦 FINAL SUBMISSION

Submit:

* your updated website (HTML, CSS, JS)

---

### 🔹 Submission Requirements

* application must be visually consistent
* interactions must be clear and understandable
* improvements must be noticeable and intentional

---

# 🔥 Instructor Notes (For You)

Students often:

* equate “more features” with “better UX”
* overlook small usability issues
* overuse styling without clarity

Your role:

* emphasize simplicity
* reward clarity over complexity
* guide them toward user-centered thinking
