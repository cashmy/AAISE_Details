# 🧭 ASSIGNMENT 6 — DEBUGGING & PROBLEM SOLVING

**Week 6 — “Things Break — And I Can Fix Them”**

---

## 🔹 Context

You have now built:

* structured pages (HTML)
* styled layouts (CSS)
* interactive behavior (JavaScript)

This week focuses on what happens when things **don’t work as expected**.

Debugging is one of the most important skills in programming.

---

## 🔹 Objective

Develop the ability to:

* identify problems
* investigate their causes
* apply structured approaches to fix them

---

# 🧱 ITERATION 1 — BUILD (Tuesday Lab)

### 🔹 Task

Work with a provided or existing version of your project that contains issues.

---

### 🔹 Requirements

You must:

* Identify at least **3 issues**, such as:

  * broken layout
  * incorrect styling
  * non-working interaction
  * console errors

* For each issue:

  * describe what is wrong
  * attempt a fix

---

### 🔹 Focus

* observing behavior
* isolating problems
* testing changes

---

### 🔹 Expectation

You may not fix everything on the first attempt.

That is expected.

---

# 🧠 CONCEPT FOCUS (Wednesday)

* Debugging as a step-by-step process
* Using the browser console to identify errors
* Tracing logic and understanding what the code is doing
* Common sources of issues across HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
* Why guessing is less effective than systematic investigation

---

# 🔧 ITERATION 2 — REFINE (Thursday Lab)

### 🔹 Task

Improve your debugging process and fix remaining issues.

---

### 🔹 Requirements

* Fix at least **2 confirmed issues completely**

* Improve your approach by:

  * using console output (`console.log`)
  * checking element selection and event flow
  * verifying layout behavior

* Clearly document:

  * what the issue was
  * how you identified it
  * how you resolved it

---

### 🔹 Focus

* systematic thinking
* clarity of process
* verification of fixes

---

# 📦 FINAL SUBMISSION

Submit:

* your updated website (HTML, CSS, JS)
* a short debugging report

---

### 🔹 Debugging Report Requirements

For each issue (minimum 2):

* What was the problem?
* How did you identify it?
* What did you change to fix it?

---

# 🧠 REFLECTION (Required — Short)

In 2–3 sentences, answer:

> How did your approach to solving problems change during this assignment?

# 🔥 Instructor Notes (For You)

This is the **confidence stabilization week**.

Students learn:

* it’s normal for things to break
* they can recover

Watch for:

* random guessing
* copy/paste fixes without understanding
* frustration turning into disengagement

Your role:

* reinforce process over speed
* reward clear thinking
* normalize failure as part of development

---

# 🧠 Subtle Concept Being Installed

> “Problems are not obstacles—they are information.”
