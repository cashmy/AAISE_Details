# 🧭 ASSIGNMENT 8 — ASYNCHRONOUS BEHAVIOR

**Week 8 — “Time Matters”**

---

## 🔹 Context

So far, your programs have run step-by-step in a predictable order.

This week, you will explore what happens when:

* actions take time
* results do not happen immediately
* systems must respond when something finishes

---

## 🔹 Objective

Understand and apply basic asynchronous behavior by:

* handling delayed actions
* working with responses that occur later
* managing when things happen, not just what happens

---
