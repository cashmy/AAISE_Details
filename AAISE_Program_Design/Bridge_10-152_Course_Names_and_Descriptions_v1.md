# Bridge `10-152` Course Names and Descriptions v1

## Purpose

This artifact provides a staff-reference draft for the finalized bridge-course
names and updated descriptions for the `10-152-nnn` sequence only.

Design constraints applied:

- bridge course names are kept at or under `42` characters
- descriptions are written to remain under `700` characters
- names avoid unnecessary use of `AI`
- descriptions are written with enough flexibility to allow content adaptation
  as technology changes
- prerequisite course relationships are included for staff reference
- semester grouping follows the current bridge design direction, including
  moved and repurposed courses from the latest bridge summary

## Working Notes

- Semester 3 reflects the current bridge decision to move `10-152-131` earlier
  so machine learning foundations occur before deep-learning work.
- Semester 4 reflects the current bridge decision to repurpose
  `10-152-133` as **Deep Learning Frameworks and Applications**.
- `10-152-125` is intentionally absent because its content is being folded into
  `10-152-122` and the standalone bridge course is being dropped.
- The final Semester 4 studio course combines the earlier emerging-technology
  and studio concepts into a single bridge-usable course because the separate
  one-credit feeder course is not part of the bridge program.
- The full proposed title "Technical Intelligence Studio for AI Engineers"
  exceeds the `42` character bridge-name limit, so the bridge-safe short form
  **Technical Intelligence Studio** is used here.
- `* 10-152-130` has `144` occupational hours in the current bridge. The draft description is written to allow for a range of applied experience types, including but not limited to workplace experience, field-based projects, simulations, service learning, capstone projects, and industry-related engagements. The final bridge design will determine the specific experience types included in the bridge offering.
