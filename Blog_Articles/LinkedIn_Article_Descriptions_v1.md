# LinkedIn Article Descriptions

## Purpose

This artifact provides short LinkedIn-ready descriptions that answer the prompt:

`Tell your network what your article is about ...`

Each description is written to pair with one of the current long-form blog articles in this series.

## 1. AI Is Not Replacing Software Expertise. It Is Repricing It.

This article argues that AI is not eliminating the need for software expertise, but changing where that expertise creates the most value. As basic app construction and routine implementation become easier to generate, the real differentiators move upward toward architecture, systems thinking, integration, and governed human judgment.

## 2. Why Software Education Must Move Beyond App Construction

This article explores why software education can no longer treat basic app construction as the main differentiator for student readiness. As AI makes simple application generation easier, curriculum value must move upward toward architecture, systems thinking, integration, data modeling, and the ability to judge AI-assisted output responsibly.

## 3. AI Adoption in Business Is Not Just a Tool Question. It Is an Architectural One.

This article examines why many organizations are still framing AI too narrowly as a productivity tool. It argues that the deeper business shift is architectural: AI changes how workflows, systems, data, governance, and human oversight need to operate together if adoption is going to create lasting value.

## 4. The Developer Is Becoming a Cognitive Architect

This article explores how AI is changing the developer role without making it disappear. As more low-level implementation work becomes easier to generate, the higher-value developer increasingly moves toward framing, orchestration, validation, integration, and the preservation of technical and architectural coherence.
