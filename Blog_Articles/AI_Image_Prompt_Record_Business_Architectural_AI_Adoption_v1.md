# AI Image Prompt Record

## Purpose

This artifact records the image concept and prompt for:

- `AI Adoption in Business Is Not Just a Tool Question. It Is an Architectural One.`

It is intended for:

- LinkedIn support imagery
- blog/article header reuse
- future business-facing AI adoption visuals

## Core Image Concept

The image should contrast two business postures toward AI.

The left side should represent shallow tool adoption:

- AI chat windows
- productivity helpers
- meeting summaries
- drafting assistance
- isolated local use

The right side should represent architectural adoption:

- workflows
- systems integration
- data movement
- approvals
- governance
- APIs
- human oversight

The visual should make clear that AI in business is not only about giving workers better tools. It is about redesigning how work, systems, and judgment operate together.

## Prompt Used

```text
Professional editorial-style LinkedIn/blog header graphic, 16:9 landscape. Split-screen composition. Left side shows shallow business AI tool use: AI chat interface, document drafting, meeting summaries, productivity dashboards, isolated assistant-style usage. Right side shows architectural AI adoption in business: workflow diagrams, system integration, APIs, data pipelines, approvals, governance checkpoints, human oversight, enterprise software layers. Visual message is that business AI adoption is not just about tools, but about architecture and workflow redesign. Premium business-technology aesthetic, clean and intelligent composition, modern executive tone, charcoal, white, muted blue, and subtle warm accents. Add elegant readable overlay title: "AI Adoption in Business Is Not Just a Tool Question. It Is an Architectural One." Smaller subtitle: "The deeper shift is in workflow, systems, and governance." No robots, no cyberpunk, no sci-fi AI brains, no stock-photo clichés, no clutter.
```

## Design Intent

The image should support the article’s core thesis:

businesses often begin by adopting AI as a productivity tool, but the deeper and more consequential shift is architectural. The value is not only in faster output, but in the redesign of workflows, integrations, and governed human-AI interaction.

## Suggested Variants

### Variant 1: LinkedIn Graphic

- preserve title and subtitle
- emphasize immediate readability in-feed

### Variant 2: Blog Header

- keep the contrast between tool adoption and architectural adoption
- reduce subtitle if cleaner composition is needed

### Variant 3: Workflow Shift Concept

- reduce literal chat/tool imagery
- emphasize movement from isolated tool use to integrated enterprise workflow design

## Style Guardrails

Avoid:

- robots
- futuristic glowing AI heads
- cliché “digital brain” art
- decorative code wallpaper
- overbusy diagrams

Prefer:

- professional editorial clarity
- executive-friendly thought leadership style
- conceptual sharpness
- modern but restrained business visual language
