# Blog Articles README

## Purpose

This folder contains a growing article series focused on AI, software expertise, software education, business adoption, developer identity, and the broader problem field that supports Refraction-Based Architecture (RBA).

The articles are not intended as isolated opinion pieces. Together, they form a structured progression:

- first naming the broad shift
- then examining its implications across education, business, and developer identity
- then opening follow-on paths for more practical implementation guidance

Several of these articles also serve a secondary purpose: they help surface and stabilize the real-world problem landscape that will support future RBA book development.

## Series Logic

The current article sequence follows a deliberate pattern:

1. Name the broad shift in value.
2. Show how that shift changes software education.
3. Show how that shift changes business AI adoption.
4. Show how that shift changes the developer role itself.

This creates a coherent progression from:

- expertise
- to education
- to enterprise adoption
- to individual technical identity

The series can later expand into more operational follow-ons, especially in the business domain.
