# 📘 Learner Profile (LP-01)

## 🔹 Entry Characteristics

* Majority: **true beginners (A)**
* Minority: some prior exposure
* Wide variance in:

  * typing fluency
  * syntax familiarity
  * problem-solving confidence

---

## 🔹 Cognitive State (Important)

Students will likely:

* seek “the right answer”
* default to copy/paste behavior
* struggle with ambiguity
* over-trust AI output (initially)

---

## 🔹 Risk Factors

* 🚩 Early frustration → disengagement
* 🚩 AI over-reliance → shallow understanding
* 🚩 Syntax overwhelm → loss of confidence
* 🚩 Comparison anxiety (mixed skill levels)

---

## 🔹 Opportunity

If guided correctly:

* AI can **reduce intimidation barrier**
* early wins can **build momentum quickly**
* structured thinking can **differentiate them early**
