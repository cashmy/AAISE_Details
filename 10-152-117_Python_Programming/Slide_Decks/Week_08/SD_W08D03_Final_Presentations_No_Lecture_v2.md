# Presentation Facilitation Notes

Recommended flow per student:

1. Project purpose.
2. Short demo.
3. Code/structure explanation.
4. Validation evidence.
5. Revision or decision explanation.
6. AI-use justification if applicable.
7. Instructor questions.

Adjust timing based on enrollment.

---

# Instructor Question Bank

Use selectively:

- What is the most important behavior in your project?
- What input does your program use?
- What output proves it worked?
- What part of the code are you most confident explaining?
- What did you test or validate?
- What changed from your original proposal?
- What would you improve with more time?
- If AI helped, what did it contribute?
- What AI suggestion did you change, reject, or verify?
- What decision remained yours?

---

# AI-Use Boundary

Students may use AI to help prepare explanation, but final answers must show
student understanding.

If a student cannot explain generated or assisted code, the instructor should
probe for what the student does understand and grade ownership accordingly.
