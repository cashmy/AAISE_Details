# ASSIGNMENT 14 — CAPSTONE PROPOSAL AND APPROVAL

**Week 7 — RBA and Project Framing**

---

# Context

Your capstone project is your opportunity to combine what you have learned into a small project of your own.

Because the course is short and the project must remain explainable, your capstone idea must be approved before major build work begins.

---

# Objective

Submit a capstone proposal that defines a realistic, explainable Python project.

Your proposal should demonstrate:

* clear scope
* feasible features
* likely structure
* risks or unknowns
* AI-use boundaries

---

# Task

Write and submit a capstone proposal for approval.

Your proposal should describe:

* project name
* project purpose
* main features
* expected inputs
* expected outputs
* likely program structure
* data use, if any
* likely risks
* AI use plan
* why the scope is realistic

---

# Requirements

Your proposal should be specific enough for instructor review.

It should show that:

* the project can be completed in the available time
* you understand what you are trying to build
* the project uses appropriate course concepts
* AI use will be bounded and disclosed

---

# AI Use

AI may be used in a bounded and disclosed way after you complete an initial project idea.

AI may help you refine scope or identify risks. AI may not choose the project for you or replace your judgment.

---

# Submission Requirements

Submit your proposal as instructed.

Your capstone may not proceed as final until the instructor approves the scope or asks for revision.

---

# Reflection / Explanation

Answer briefly:

> Why is this project realistic for the time and skills available in this course?

If you used AI:

> How did AI help refine the proposal, and what final decisions were yours?

# Instructor Notes

This is the scope-control checkpoint.

Students may overreach. Help them reduce feature count while preserving a meaningful project.

The key learning target is:

> a project is more likely to succeed when scope, structure, and constraints are explicit before building.
