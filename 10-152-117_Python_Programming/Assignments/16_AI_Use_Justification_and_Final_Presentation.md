# ASSIGNMENT 16 — AI USE JUSTIFICATION AND FINAL PRESENTATION

**Week 8 — Capstone Build, Justification, and Presentation**

---

# Context

A finished project is not only something that runs.

You must also be able to explain what it does, why you built it the way you did, how you tested it, and how AI was used if it was part of your process.

This assignment completes the capstone through presentation and accountability.

---

# Objective

Present your capstone project and submit an AI use justification when required.

Your work should demonstrate:

* clear explanation
* project ownership
* testing and validation awareness
* accountable AI use
* professional presentation

---

# Task

Present your final capstone project.

Your presentation should include:

* what you built
* what problem or purpose it addresses
* how the program works
* what course concepts it uses
* how you tested or validated it
* what you would improve with more time
* how AI was used, if applicable

---

# Requirements

You must:

* demonstrate the project
* explain the main logic and structure
* discuss testing or validation
* submit the AI use justification if AI was used or if required by the instructor
* answer instructor questions about your project

---

# AI Use

AI may be used to help prepare wording, organize explanation, or polish presentation materials if allowed.

AI may not replace your understanding. You must be able to answer questions about the final project and explain the role AI played.

---

# Submission Requirements

Submit as instructed:

* final project files
* presentation or demo materials if required
* AI use justification document, if required

---

# Reflection / Explanation

Answer briefly:

> What was the most important decision you made during this project, and why?

If AI was used:

> What did AI contribute, what did you change or reject, and what decisions remained yours?
