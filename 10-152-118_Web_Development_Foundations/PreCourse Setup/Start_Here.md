## **Weekly Class Format**

Most weeks in this course follow a steady build-and-refine rhythm:

* **Monday** — Lecture
  * Reading assignment is due before class
* **Tuesday** — Lab / Homework
  * Based on the Monday lecture
* **Wednesday** — Video Lecture / Demo
  * Focused on refinement and iteration
* **Thursday** — Lab / Homework
  * Based on the Wednesday video

This means the course is designed to help you build something, then improve it, rather than trying to do everything at once.
