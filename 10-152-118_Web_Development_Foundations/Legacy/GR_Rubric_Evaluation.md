# 📘 HTML, CSS & JavaScript — Evaluation System (GR-01 / AMM-01)

---

# 🔹 PART 1 — STANDARD LAB RUBRIC (WEEKS 1–14)

---

## 🔹 Category 1 — Structure (HTML) — 20%

**Exceeds (A)**

* Clean, semantic HTML
* Logical structure across pages
* Proper linking/navigation

**Meets (B)**

* Mostly correct structure with minor issues

**Developing (C)**

* Inconsistent or unclear structure

**Needs Improvement (D/F)**

* Broken or incorrect structure

---

## 🔹 Category 2 — Styling & Layout (CSS) — 20%

**Exceeds**

* Intentional use of spacing, typography, and layout
* Visually clear and readable
* Consistent across pages

**Meets**

* Functional styling with minor inconsistencies

**Developing**

* Basic styling, limited control

**Needs Improvement**

* Poor readability or layout issues

---

## 🔹 Category 3 — Interactivity (JavaScript) — 15%

*(Introduced Weeks 10+)*

**Exceeds**

* Correct use of logic and events
* Behavior matches intended outcome

**Meets**

* Basic interactivity works

**Developing**

* Partial or inconsistent behavior

**Needs Improvement**

* Broken or missing functionality

---

## 🔹 Category 4 — Debugging & Problem Solving — 15%

**Exceeds**

* Identifies and fixes issues independently
* Uses appropriate debugging methods (visual → console → tools)

**Meets**

* Fixes issues with some guidance

**Developing**

* Struggles to identify issues

**Needs Improvement**

* Unable to debug

---

## 🔹 Category 5 — Explanation & Understanding — 15%

**Exceeds**

* Clearly explains:

  * structure
  * styling decisions
  * behavior

**Meets**

* General understanding

**Developing**

* Limited explanation

**Needs Improvement**

* Cannot explain work

---

## 🔹 Category 6 — Effort, Iteration & Improvement — 15%

*(Confidence-protection category)*

**Exceeds**

* Shows refinement and improvement
* Applies feedback

**Meets**

* Completes required work

**Developing**

* Minimal effort

**Needs Improvement**

* Incomplete or missing work

---

# 🔹 PART 2 — AI USAGE CATEGORY (WEEKS 14–16 ONLY)

---

## 🔹 Category 7 — AI Integration (Late Phase Only)

**Exceeds**

* Uses AI intentionally to improve design or functionality
* Compares AI vs manual approaches
* Maintains control over output

**Meets**

* Uses AI appropriately

**Developing**

* Over-reliance or limited understanding

**Needs Improvement**

* Blind copying

---

👉 Important:

> AI is NOT graded early
> AI is ONLY evaluated when introduced

---

# 🔹 PART 3 — FINAL PROJECT RUBRIC (WEEKS 15–16)

---

## 🔹 Additional Categories

---

### 🔹 Integration (NEW)

**Exceeds**

* Combines HTML, CSS, JS cleanly
* Feels like a complete site

**Meets**

* All components present

**Developing**

* Partial integration

**Needs Improvement**

* Fragmented

---

### 🔹 Design Quality (NEW — Important)

*(Handled carefully to avoid discouragement)*

**Exceeds**

* Clear visual hierarchy
* Good spacing and readability
* Consistent design

**Meets**

* Functional and readable

**Developing**

* Basic design, lacks refinement

**Needs Improvement**

* Difficult to use or read

---

👉 Note:

This evaluates:

> **clarity and usability—not artistic talent**

---

### 🔹 Independence vs AI Balance

**Exceeds**

* Strong manual core
* AI used for refinement

**Meets**

* Balanced use

**Developing**

* Over-reliance on one

**Needs Improvement**

* No independent capability

# 🔹 PART 4 — QUIZ MODEL

---

## 🔹 Focus Areas

* HTML structure recognition
* CSS behavior understanding
* JavaScript logic prediction
* debugging reasoning

---

## 🔹 Example Types

* “What will this layout look like?”
* “Why is this not displaying correctly?”
* “What does this JavaScript do?”

---

👉 Avoid:

* memorization of tags
* syntax trivia

# 🔹 PART 6 — INSTRUCTOR CALIBRATION GUIDE

---

## 🔹 What to Reward

* clarity
* improvement
* usability
* understanding

---

## 🔹 What NOT to Over-Penalize

* imperfect design aesthetics
* minor layout inconsistencies
* early CSS struggles

---

---

## 🔹 What to Watch

### 🚩 “It works but looks chaotic”

→ teach structure + spacing

---

### 🚩 “Looks good but is fragile”

→ reinforce debugging

---

### 🚩 “Copied layout”

→ require explanation

---

### 🚩 “Fear of design”

→ simplify and guide

---

# 🔹 PART 7 — GRADING PHILOSOPHY

* usability
* understanding
