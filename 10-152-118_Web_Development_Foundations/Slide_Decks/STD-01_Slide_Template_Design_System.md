# 🧭 **SLIDE TEMPLATE DESIGN System (v1.2 — FULL & ATOMIC)**

*SWTC — Concept-Driven Instruction*

---

# 🧱 1. CORE PRINCIPLE (Non-Negotiable)

> Slides are **visual anchors**, not content containers.

---

## Implications

* One idea per slide
* No paragraphs
* No explanations on slides
* Slides trigger thinking, not deliver content
* Slides must be **memorable at a glance**

---

# 🧠 2. THREE-LAYER MODEL (Canonical Structure)

Every slide must include all three layers:

---

## 🔹 1. Slide (Student Layer)

**Definition:**

* The visible text on the slide
* The cognitive anchor

**Characteristics:**

* Minimal
* Memorable
* Declarative
* One idea only

---

## 🔹 2. Cue (Instructor Layer)

**Definition:**

* How the slide is delivered

**Includes:**

* pacing (pause, slow down, reinforce)
* emphasis (what matters most)
* framing (how to position idea)

---

## 🔹 3. Visual Intent (Catalyst Layer) ✅

**Definition:**

> A structured directive describing:

* how the idea is visually represented
* what is shown
* why it matters

---

## 🔥 Critical Rule

> If Visual Intent is unclear in layout, content, or purpose → the slide is incomplete.

---

# 🧱 3. SLIDE SPEC FORMAT (Standardized)

Use this exact structure:

```text
### SLIDE X — [TYPE]

[Primary Line — anchor]
[Optional second line — contrast or constraint]

---
Cue:
- (delivery guidance)
- (emphasis, pacing, framing)

Visual:
- Layout:
- Content:
  - (element 1)
  - (element 2)
- Purpose:
  - (what this reveals)
```

---

# 🧭 4. SLIDE ARCHETYPES (Instructional Primitives)

Limit to **7–8 archetypes maximum**

---

## 🔹 1. OPENING FRAME

**Purpose:**

* Set emotional tone
* Define direction

**Structure:**

* Line 1: Declarative anchor
* Line 2: Constraint or contrast

**Example:**

> Build something that exists.
> Not something impressive.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Split contrast (nothing vs something)

---

## 🔹 2. CORE IDEA

**Purpose:**

* Define a concept clearly

**Structure:**

* Line 1: What it is
* Line 2: What it is not (optional)

**Example:**

> HTML defines meaning.
> Not appearance.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Structure vs styling comparison

---

## 🔹 3. CONTRAST

**Purpose:**

* Install judgment / decision rule

**Structure:**

* X beats Y
* Optional reinforcement

**Example:**

> Working beats pretty.
> Every time.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Split comparison

---

## 🔹 4. SYSTEM

**Purpose:**

* Show relationships between components

**Structure:**

* System statement
* Optional clarifier

**Example:**

> Pages are connected.
> Not isolated.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Nodes + arrows

---

## 🔹 5. COMMON FAILURE

**Purpose:**

* Preempt predictable mistakes

**Structure:**

* Statement of mistake

**Example:**

> Trying to make it look good too early.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Overcomplicated vs simple

---

## 🔹 6. THINKING TOOL (Heuristic)

**Purpose:**

* Install internal evaluation pattern

**Structure:**

* Question

**Example:**

> Can I explain what this section is?

**Visual Pattern:**

* Highlighted sections

---

## 🔹 7. BRIDGE (Lab / Assignment / Transition)

**Purpose:**

* Direct action

**Structure:**

* Action
* Optional constraint

**Example:**

> Build a multi-page site.
> Make it work.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Simple system diagram

---

## 🔹 8. CLOSING (Optional but recommended)

**Purpose:**

* Reinforce success definition

**Structure:**

* If X → success

**Example:**

> If it works, you succeeded.

**Visual Pattern:**

* Simple successful example

---

# 🧱 5. TEXT RULES (Strict)

---

## 🔹 Line Limits

* Maximum: **2 lines per slide**
* Preferred: **1 line**

---

## 🔹 Word Limits

* Target: **3–8 words per line**
* Hard maximum: **10 words per line**

---

## 🔹 Language Rules

Use:

* simple words
* direct phrasing
* declarative tone

Avoid:

* jargon
* filler words
* hedging language

---

## 🔹 Emphasis (Handled in PPT Template)

* Line 1 → dominant
* Line 2 → secondary

---

# 🧱 6. VISUAL INTENT SYSTEM (Formalized v1.2)

---

## 🔹 Definition

> Visual Intent defines:

* spatial arrangement (**Layout**)
* elements shown (**Content**)
* instructional purpose (**Purpose**)

---

## 🔹 Required Format

```text
Visual:
- Layout: (spatial structure)
- Content:
  - (element 1)
  - (element 2)
- Purpose:
  - (what this reveals)
```

---

## 🔹 Visual Layout Types (Standard Library)

---

### 1. Split (Contrast)

* left vs right
* comparison

---

### 2. Stack (Layers)

* vertical layers
* foundation → top

---

### 3. Flow (Sequence)

* arrows
* directional movement

---

### 4. Hierarchy (Structure)

* nested
* indented

---

### 5. Before / After (State Change)

* same object
* different states

---

### 6. Isolated Focus

* single element emphasized

---

## 🔹 Visual Rules

* One visual concept per slide
* Must reinforce the idea
* No decorative visuals
* Must be executable without guessing

---

## 🔹 Diagnostic Rule (Critical)

> If you cannot define Layout + Content + Purpose clearly → the slide needs refinement.

---

# 🧱 7. SPEAKER NOTE STRUCTURE

Each slide contains:

---

## 🔹 Cue

* delivery guidance
* pacing
* emphasis

---

## 🔹 Visual

* what to show
* how to show it

---

## 🔹 Intent (Optional)

* why the slide exists
* what it installs cognitively

---

# 🧱 8. LECTURE COMPOSITION RULES

---

## 🔹 Slide Count

* Target: **7–8 slides**
* Maximum: **10 slides**

---

## 🔹 Recommended Flow

1. Opening Frame
2. Core Idea
3. Core Idea / System
4. Contrast / Failure
5. Visual Reinforcement / Demo
6. Thinking Tool
7. Bridge
8. Closing

---

## 🔹 Density Rule

> If you need more than 10 slides → you are over-teaching.

---

# 🧱 9. PPT TEMPLATE DESIGN (Implementation Layer)

---

## 🔹 Required Layouts (Create 5)

1. Single line (centered)
2. Two-line anchor
3. Split (text + visual)
4. Full visual + overlay text
5. Minimal emphasis

---

## 🔹 Typography

* Large
* High contrast
* Clean spacing

---

## 🔹 Color System

* 1 primary color
* 1 accent color
* neutral background

---

# 🧠 10. QUALITY CHECK (Pre-Use Validation)

Before presenting any deck:

* Is there exactly one idea per slide?
* Is the phrasing memorable?
* Is Visual Intent fully defined?
* Are cues actionable?
* Is anything unnecessary?
