# 🧭 ASSIGNMENT 17 — CAPSTONE PRESENTATION

**Week 17 — “Explain Your System”**

---

## 🔹 Context

You have now:

* designed your own application
* built and refined your system
* applied the concepts from the course

This week, you will present your work and explain how your system functions.

---

## 🔹 Objective

Demonstrate your understanding by:

* presenting your application
* explaining how it works
* describing decisions you made during development

---

# 🧱 PRESENTATION TASK

### 🔹 Requirements

You will present your project to the class.

Your presentation must include:

---

## 1. Project Overview

* What did you build?
* What problem does it solve or what purpose does it serve?

---

## 2. System Demonstration

* Show your application working
* Walk through key features

---

## 3. How It Works

Explain:

* how your HTML structure supports your system
* how CSS affects layout and usability
* how JavaScript controls behavior

---

## 4. Key Concepts Used

Describe at least **2–3 concepts**, such as:

* interactivity (events, DOM)
* state management
* data handling (JSON, API, or input)
* debugging or problem-solving

---

## 5. Challenges & Solutions

* What was difficult?
* How did you solve it?

---

## 6. Improvements (Optional)

* What would you add or improve if you had more time?

---

# 🎤 PRESENTATION FORMAT

* 5–7 minutes per student (or group)
* Screen sharing required
* Live demonstration expected

---

# 🧠 REFLECTION (Required — Short)
