# Week 08 Success Notes - Asynchronous Behavior

This package shows one acceptable successful version for Assignment 8. It continues the `Study Sprint` planner by adding visible timing and delayed feedback.

## Files Included

- `index.html`
- `tips.html`
- `schedule.html`
- `styles.css`
- `script.js`

## What This Version Demonstrates

- delayed behavior using `setTimeout()`
- clear waiting and completion messages
- user-visible timing behavior
- organized functions
- the existing planner behavior still works

## Instructor Demo Use

Show this at the beginning of Week 9 after Assignment 8 has been submitted.

Recommended rhythm:

1. Open `index.html`.
2. Use the study planner normally.
3. Click the delayed reminder button.
4. Point out what happens immediately and what happens later.
5. Connect the behavior to the Assignment 8 requirement that timing must be visible and intentional.

## Revision Recovery Connection

Students revising Assignment 8 should focus on making the delayed behavior visible and understandable. The revision note should explain what happens now, what happens later, and how the student verified the timing.

## Tradeoffs

This version uses `setTimeout()` rather than `fetch()` because the assignment can be completed with delayed behavior alone. Data retrieval is introduced in Week 10.
