# Web Development Foundations Success Solutions

This folder contains instructor-facing success solution packages for selected assignments.

## Purpose

Each package shows one acceptable completed version of an assignment. It is not the only correct answer.

These are intended for use after the original submission cycle, usually at the beginning of the following week. They help students who struggled see a complete result, compare it to their own work, and revise meaningfully when revision recovery is allowed.

## Relationship To Other Course Materials

- `Demos/` are built live during lecture or recording.
- `Assignments/Success_Solutions/` are final reference outcomes shown after students have attempted the assignment.
- `Course_Materials/` provide handouts and conceptual support.
- PageForge remains a separate instructor-only iterative model.

## Revision Recovery Use

When using a success solution for revision recovery, remind students that copying the example is not the goal.

Students should revise their own work and include a short note:

```text
What I changed:

Why I changed it:

How I verified the improvement:
```
