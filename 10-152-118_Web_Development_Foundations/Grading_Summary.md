# Course 10-152-118: Web Development Foundations Grading Summary

Each assignment may be assessed in up to **11 rubric categories**. Each category is scored from **0 to 4 points**, so a fully assessed assignment is worth up to **44 points**.

Your course grade includes **17 assignments** organized into two categories:

| Category | Assignments | Weight in final grade |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Homework/Lab | A1-A15 (15 assignments) | 78.95% |
| Capstone Submission | A16-A17 (2 assignments) | 21.05% |

The Capstone Build and Capstone Presentation assignments each count twice as much as a regular assignment in your final grade.
