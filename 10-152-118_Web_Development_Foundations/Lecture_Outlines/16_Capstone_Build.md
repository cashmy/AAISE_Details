# 🧭 **WEEK 16 — MONDAY LECTURE OUTLINE**

**Title:** *Week 16 — Capstone Build: Executing Your System*

---

## **1. Opening Frame**

> “You now have an approved idea.
> This week, you begin building your final system.”

* Shift from guided work → owned work
* Set tone: **focused execution**

---

## **2. Course Positioning**

> “You’ve learned all the pieces.
> Now you apply them together to build something complete.”

* connects to:

  * Week 11 (integration)
  * Week 12–14 (stability, reliability, UX)
  * Week 15 (input + idea selection)

---

## **3. Core Concepts**

* Breaking work into manageable pieces
* Building core features first
* Iteration under independence
* Managing complexity
* Using tools (AI, libraries) intentionally

---

## **4. Demonstration / Example Layer**

* Show:

  * breaking a project into parts
* Show:

  * simple feature → built step-by-step
* Show:

  * prioritizing core vs optional features

👉 Emphasize:

* progress over perfection

---

## **5. Common Mistakes / Watch-Fors**

* trying to build everything at once
* over-scoping
* getting stuck on one issue
* losing structure

---

## **6. Tuesday Lab Bridge**

> “In lab, your goal is to get your core system started.”

Focus:

* basic structure
* initial functionality
* working foundation

---

## **7. Closing Frame**

> “Start simple, get something working, then improve.”

# 🧭 **WEEK 16 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 16 — Managing Complexity & Progress*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about building steadily—not perfectly.”

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* Large systems are built incrementally
* Progress comes from small steps
* Debugging continues throughout
* Independence requires structure

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “I don’t know where to start” → unclear breakdown
* “This is too much” → over-scoping
* “I’m stuck” → lack of iteration

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

* “What is the smallest working version?”
* “What is the next step?”
* “What is blocking me?”
* “Can I simplify this?”

---

## **5. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, focus on improving and stabilizing what you’ve built.”

Examples:

* fix issues
* refine logic
* improve usability

---

## **6. Closing Reinforcement**

> “Progress is built step-by-step—not all at once.”
