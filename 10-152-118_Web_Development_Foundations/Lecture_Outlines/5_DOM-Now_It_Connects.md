# 🧭 **WEEK 5 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 5 — Events, Interaction, and Feedback*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about making your page respond to actions.”

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* Events trigger behavior
* JavaScript reacts to user input
* The page is no longer static
* Interaction creates experience

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “Nothing happens when I click” → event not attached
* “Wrong thing changes” → selector issue
* “It works sometimes” → timing or logic issue

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

* “What triggers this?”
* “What element am I targeting?”
* “What should happen after the event?”
* “Can I see the change clearly?”

---

## **5. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, improve clarity and reliability of your interaction.”

Examples:

* make behavior consistent
* improve feedback (clear result)
* clean up logic

---

## **6. Closing Reinforcement**

> “Interaction turns a page into an application.”
