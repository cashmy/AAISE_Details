# 🧭 **WEEK 15 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 15 — Input as Intent & Idea Exploration*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about turning user input into meaningful intent.”

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* Input represents user intent
* Systems interpret and act on that intent
* Good input design improves clarity
* Input systems shape what your application can do

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “What input do I actually need?”
* “Why is my form unclear?”
* “How does this connect to my application?”

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

* “What is the user trying to do?”
* “What input is required to support that?”
* “Is this input clear and usable?”
* “What happens after input is submitted?”

---

## **5. Capstone Idea Introduction (LIGHTWEIGHT)**

> “You will begin thinking about what you want to build—but you are not designing it yet.”

Clarify:

* You will propose **2–3 ideas**
* These are:

  * exploratory
  * adjustable
  * subject to approval

---

## **6. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, focus on improving your input system and preparing your idea proposals.”

Examples:

* improve form clarity
* refine input handling
* draft 2–3 possible ideas

---

## **7. Closing Reinforcement**

> “Good systems start with clear input—and clear ideas.”
