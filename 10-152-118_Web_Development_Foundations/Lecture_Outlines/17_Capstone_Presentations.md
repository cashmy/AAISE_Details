# 🧭 **WEEK 17 — MONDAY LECTURE OUTLINE**

---

## **1. Opening Frame**

> “You’ve built your system.
> Now you will demonstrate and explain it.”

* Shift from building → communicating
* Set tone: clarity over perfection

---

## **2. Course Positioning**

> “This is the final step—showing not just what works, but what you understand.”

* connects to:

  * all prior weeks
* reinforces:

  * integration
  * clarity
  * ownership

---

## **3. Core Concepts**

* Explaining vs showing
* Communicating clearly:

  * what it does
  * how it works
* Demonstrating functionality
* Describing decisions
* Reflecting on challenges

---

## **4. Demonstration / Example Layer**

* Show:

  * example presentation structure
* Show:

  * clear vs unclear explanation

👉 Emphasize:

* simple explanation
* structured flow

---

## **5. Common Mistakes / Watch-Fors**

* only showing features (not explaining)
* unclear or disorganized presentation
* reading from notes
* over-explaining details

---

## **6. Tuesday Lab / Prep Bridge**

> “In this session, your goal is to prepare your presentation.”

Focus:

* organize explanation
* rehearse demonstration
* clarify key points

---

## **7. Closing Frame**

> “If you can explain your system clearly, you understand it.”
