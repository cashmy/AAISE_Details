# 🧭 **WEEK 1 — MONDAY LECTURE OUTLINE**

**Title:** *Week 1 — Structure: Creating Something That Exists*

---

## **1. Opening Frame**

> “This week, we are not trying to build something impressive.
> We are trying to build something that exists—and works.”

* Introduce idea: existence before perfection
* Reduce pressure immediately

---

## **2. Course Positioning**

> “Over this course, you will build a system step-by-step.
> This week is the foundation—structure.”

* HTML = structure
* CSS = appearance (later)
* JS = behavior (later)

👉 Establish separation early (important for your philosophy)

---

## **3. Core Concepts**

* What a web page actually is (document, not “app” yet)
* HTML as structure (not styling)
* Elements and hierarchy (headings, paragraphs, lists)
* Multi-page thinking (not just one page)
* Navigation as connection between pages

---

## **4. Demonstration / Example Layer**

* Show:

  * simple HTML page (unstyled)
  * same page with clear structure vs messy structure

* Show:

  * multiple pages connected via links

👉 Emphasize:

* “this works” vs “this looks good”

---

## **5. Common Mistakes / Watch-Fors**

* Trying to make it look good (premature CSS thinking)
* Putting everything in one page
* Broken links (file names don’t match)
* Ignoring structure (just dumping content)

---

## **6. Tuesday Lab Bridge**

> “In lab, your goal is not design.
> Your goal is to create a working multi-page structure.”

Focus them on:

* pages exist
* navigation works
* content is organized

---

## **7. Closing Frame**

> “If your site works—even if it’s ugly—you succeeded this week.”

---

# 🧭 **WEEK 1 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 1 — Thinking in Structure*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about structure—not appearance.”

Reinforce separation again.

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* HTML is meaning, not presentation
* Structure communicates intent
* Pages are connected, not isolated
* Hierarchy matters (h1 vs h2 vs paragraphs)

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “Why does my page look bad?” → (because styling hasn’t been introduced)
* “Why isn’t my link working?” → path/file mismatch
* “Everything is technically there but feels wrong” → poor structure

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

Give them simple checks:

* “Can I explain what this section is?”
* “Do my headings make sense if read alone?”
* “Can I navigate between pages without confusion?”

---

## **5. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, focus on improving structure—not adding features.”

Specifically:

* fix navigation
* improve hierarchy
* clean up content organization

---

## **6. Closing Reinforcement**

> “If someone can move through your site and understand it, your structure is working.”

---

# 🧠 Instructor Notes (For You)

Week 1 is about:

* reducing anxiety
* preventing overreach
* installing discipline: structure first

---

## Watch for:

* students trying to jump ahead (CSS, JS)
* frustration about “ugliness”
* broken navigation

---

## Reinforce constantly:

> “Working > pretty”

---

# 🔑 Why This Week Is Critical

If Week 1 is done right:

* Week 2 (CSS) becomes meaningful
* Week 3 (layout) becomes logical
* Week 5 (JS) has something to attach to

If not:

* everything becomes fragile
