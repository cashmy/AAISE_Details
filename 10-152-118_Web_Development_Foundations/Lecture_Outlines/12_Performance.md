**Title:** *Week 12 — Stabilization: Strengthening Your System*

---

## **1. Opening Frame**

> “You now have all the pieces and can connect them.
> This week, we focus on making your system stable and reliable.”

* Shift from building → strengthening

---

## **2. Course Positioning**

> “Before you begin your capstone, your current system needs to be solid.”

* connects to:

  * Week 6 (debugging)
  * Week 7 (structure)
  * Week 11 (integration)

---

## **3. Core Concepts**

* Stability vs functionality
* Reviewing system behavior:

  * what works
  * what breaks
* Identifying weak points
* Simplifying where needed
* Preparing for extension

---

## **4. Demonstration / Example Layer**

* Show:

  * working but fragile system
* Show:

  * same system improved for clarity/stability

👉 Emphasize:

* reliability matters more than features

---

* adding features instead of fixing issues
* ignoring small bugs
* unclear structure
* overcomplicating solutions

---

## **6. Tuesday Lab Bridge**

> “In lab, your goal is to improve your existing system—not expand it.”

Focus:

* fix bugs
* improve clarity
* stabilize behavior

---

## **7. Closing Frame**

> “A stable system is one you can build on with confidence.”

# 🧭 **WEEK 12 — WEDNESDAY CONCEPT FOCUS**

**Title:** *Week 12 — Reliability and Preparation*

---

## **1. Re-Anchor the Week**

> “This week is about making your system dependable.”

---

## **2. Deep Concept Focus**

* Reliability as a design goal
* Systems should behave predictably
* Simplicity improves stability
* Preparation reduces future problems

---

## **3. Common Confusion Patterns**

* “It mostly works” → hidden instability
* “I’ll fix it later” → compounded issues
* “More features will fix it” → incorrect approach

---

## **4. Thinking Tools / Heuristics**

* “What breaks under different inputs?”
* “Is this behavior consistent?”
* “Can I explain how this works?”
* “Is this more complex than necessary?”

---

## **5. Thursday Refinement Bridge**

> “For Thursday, focus on making your system predictable and clear.”

Examples:

* test different inputs
* simplify logic
* fix inconsistencies

---

## **6. Closing Reinforcement**

> “Reliable systems are easier to extend and improve.”

---
