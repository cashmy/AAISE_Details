# Week 9 Image Generation Prompts

**10-152-118 Web Development Foundations**

---

# Purpose

This companion artifact expands the image notes from the Week 9 deck sources
into explicit image-generation prompts for browser-based ChatGPT image
generation.

Primary workflow:

- Copy one prompt block at a time into the browser-based ChatGPT image tool.
- Use Codex image generation only as a fallback if the browser image result is
  significantly unusable.
- Do not generate images for `What Counts As Success Today`; use the standard
  course SmartArt / built-in graphic pattern for that slide.
- Preserve slide numbering as placement guidance for the final PowerPoint
  sequence.
- Week 9 visuals should distinguish modularity from modules, show purposeful
  responsibility boundaries, and make multi-file organization feel practical
  rather than advanced or abstract.

Global style guidance:

- clean instructional PowerPoint visual
- white or very light background
- developed classroom slide composition with distinct title, label, and caption hierarchy
- fine icon strokes, meaningful layering, subtle depth, and balanced information density
- subject-appropriate detail: refined diagrams, polished worksheets, realistic toolboxes
- large readable labels
- restrained decoration; preserve useful illustration and material detail
- restrained SWTC-friendly colors: navy, teal, warm gold, white, soft gray
- no dense code walls
- no dark terminal imagery
- no cyber-security or hacker aesthetic
- no framework, package manager, or React branding
- no magical AI replacement imagery
- no tangled enterprise architecture diagrams

Source decks:

- `W09A_Modular_Thinking_Live.md`
- `W09B_Multi_File_Organization_Recorded.md`

## Visual Continuity Observation And Guidance

Observed October 6, 2026: Week 7 images generated in early July used richer
complete slide compositions, finer illustration, varied typography, and more
material detail than recent Week 8 outputs under similar style instructions.
Similar simplification was reported in current browser-based ChatGPT tests.
This records a visual difference, not a verified model or application change.

Week 8 Wednesday pilots restored the desired treatment through explicit
composition requirements and attached Week 7 references. The diagram pilot
needed one edit to remove an unrelated bridge copied from its reference;
the toolbox pilot restored realistic tools, foam texture, hardware, and depth.
These observations inform the prompts below. Week 9 outputs have not yet
been generated or visually verified under this revised guidance.

Use an appropriate Week 7 image as a style reference. Paths are relative to
this artifact:

- Diagrams: `../Slide_Decks/Images/Week_07/07-01-03_Debugging_vs_Refactoring.png`
- Toolbox: `../Slide_Decks/Images/Week_07/07-01-05_Todays_Toolbox.png`
- Worksheet: `../Slide_Decks/Images/Week_07/07-02-15_AI_Prompt_Pattern.png`

Borrow typography, composition, stroke weight, lighting, and material treatment.
Do not copy unrelated reference objects, wording, example values, or claims.
Each copyable prompt includes its own visual treatment. Its section title is
allowed as the image title; retain its existing labels, syntax, and captions.
Do not invent subtitles, examples, or explanations. Extra instructional text
requires approval. Use an existing caption or reminder in a takeaway band;
when none is supplied, omit the band rather than manufacture a claim.

Generate one image at a time. Inspect text, concept accuracy, hierarchy,
reference-content leakage, and slide-scale readability before proceeding.
Use a targeted revision when an image copies unrelated reference content or
falls back to oversized pictograms. Preserve required relationships and values.
