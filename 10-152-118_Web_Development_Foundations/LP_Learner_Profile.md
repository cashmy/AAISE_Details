# 🔹 Now — CDP Step 2 (Learner Profile)

---

## 📘 Learner Profile (LP-01) — HTML/CSS/JS

### 🔹 Entry Characteristics

* Mixed audience:

  * program students
  * possible stand-alone “web designer” learners
* Likely assumptions:

  * “This will be easier than programming”
  * “This is more visual, less technical”

---

### 🔹 Cognitive State

Students will:

* feel confident early (HTML)
* become uncertain (CSS)
* hit friction again (JavaScript)

---

### 🔹 Risk Patterns

* 🚩 CSS frustration (“why doesn’t this work?”)
* 🚩 layout confusion
* 🚩 over-reliance on copying snippets
* 🚩 aesthetic insecurity (“mine doesn’t look good”)

---

### 🔹 Opportunity

* visible output → strong motivation
* fast feedback loop (browser)
* creative ownership

---

# 🔹 Step 3 — Outcome Identity (OIS-01)

---

## 📘 Outcome Identity Statement

> **After this course, students are able to design and build a small multi-page website using HTML, CSS, and basic JavaScript, while understanding and explaining how structure, styling, and interactivity work, and applying AI tools appropriately to support development.**

---

### 🔹 Expanded (For You)

Students can:

* structure content (HTML)
* style it intentionally (CSS)
* add basic interaction (JS)
* debug visually and logically
* explain what they built
* use AI *after* forming their own approach

---

### 🔹 Hidden Outcome (Critical)

> They develop **taste + control**

This is different from Python.

---

# 🔹 Key Design Identity (This Course Is…)

Let’s name it clearly:

> **“Structured Creativity with Technical Control”**
