# 17. Design Evolution

## Why This Section Exists

Captures the reasoning behind the final program design.

### Initial Concept
Robot-focused technology camp.

### Major Constraints
- $500 budget
- 40 students
- 2.5-hour sessions
- Shared equipment model
- Limited prep timeline

### Key Discoveries
- Prompt cards reduce startup friction.
- Shared robot actors outperform individual ownership.
- Human agency became the central educational theme.
- Student packets provide continuity and reflection.
- Parent messaging became an important secondary outcome.

### Final Vision

A creative studio experience where students use AI, storytelling, music, narration, and robotics to learn that humans direct technology.
